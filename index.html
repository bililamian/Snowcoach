Snow Coach（以下简称「本平台」或「我们」）是面向大多伦多地区滑雪教练与雪友的信息平台。本政策说明我们在您使用 Snow Coach iOS 应用时收集哪些信息、如何使用、与谁共享，以及您享有的权利。我们遵守加拿大《个人信息保护和电子文件法》（PIPEDA）。

本平台仅面向年满 18 周岁的用户。我们不会有意收集未成年人的个人信息。

二、我们如何使用这些信息

  • 提供核心功能：展示教练档案与拼车信息、建立聊天、计算大致距离（基于邮编前三位对应的地区中心点，仅为估算）。
  • 发送通知：新消息、拼车被取消或被移出等提醒。
  • 核实 App 内购买并开通相应服务。
  • 维护平台安全：自动过滤不当用语，处理举报，处理违规账号。
  • 回复您的反馈并改进产品。

我们不出售您的个人信息，也不将其用于广告。

三、其他用户可以看到的信息

  • 您的昵称和头像对与您互动的用户可见。
  • 已上线的教练档案（含照片与社交媒体链接）对所有用户可见，其他用户可看到与您的大致距离。
  • 您发布的拼车信息对所有用户可见；加入拼车后，您的昵称和头像对同车成员可见。
  • 聊天消息仅对该会话的成员可见。

四、我们与谁共享信息

我们仅与提供必要服务的以下服务商共享信息，并仅限于完成相应服务所需：

  • Supabase：数据库、账号认证与文件存储。数据存储于美国（俄勒冈州）的服务器。
  • Apple：Apple 登录、推送通知（通知内容包括消息正文的前 120 个字，或拼车相关提醒中的车主昵称、出发时间和雪场，可能显示在锁屏上）、App 内购买。
  • Resend：邮件发送服务，用于将举报与意见反馈的内容发送至我们的邮箱。其服务器位于美国。

除上述情形外，我们只会在法律要求、应政府机关合法要求，或为保护用户人身安全所必需时披露信息。

跨境传输：由于上述服务商位于美国，您的信息将在加拿大境外存储和处理，并可能受当地法律约束，包括当地执法机关依法调取。

五、安全

数据传输全程使用加密连接（HTTPS / TLS）。数据库按用户设置访问权限，用户只能读写自己有权访问的数据。聊天消息不是端到端加密，在处理举报或依法要求时我们可能查看相关内容。任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。

六、保存期限与删除账号

您可以随时在应用内「我的 → 删除账号」永久删除账号。删除后：

  • 您的账号、个人资料、头像、教练档案及照片、发布的拼车、收藏、拉黑记录、推送令牌、意见反馈将被删除。
  • 如您通过 Apple 登录，我们会同时向 Apple 撤销本应用的登录授权。
  • 您已发送的聊天消息将与您的账号解除关联，在对方的会话中显示为已注销的用户。
  • 您提交的举报会保留（不再关联您的账号），用于平台安全处理。
  • 购买的交易记录会保留（不再关联您的账号），用于防止重复发放及财务记录。

账号存续期间，我们会一直保存上述信息，以提供服务。

七、您的权利

依据 PIPEDA，您有权查阅我们持有的您的个人信息、要求更正不准确的信息，以及撤回同意（撤回同意可能导致无法继续使用部分或全部功能）。大部分信息可直接在应用内修改。如需查阅或有其他请求，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们将在 30 天内答复。如您对我们的处理不满意，可以向加拿大隐私专员办公室（Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada，priv.gc.ca）投诉。

八、政策变更

本政策如有重大变更，我们将在应用内通知您，并更新本页顶部的生效日期。

Snow Coach ("we", "us") is an information platform connecting ski and snowboard coaches and riders in the Greater Toronto Area. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Snow Coach iOS app, how we use and share it, and your rights. We comply with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If the Chinese and English versions differ, the Chinese version prevails.

Snow Coach is intended only for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

We do not collect precise location (no GPS, no location permission), contacts, or your photo library (photos are chosen with the system picker and only the photos you select are uploaded). We do not use the advertising identifier, track you across apps, or include any advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the service: show coach profiles and rides, enable chat, and estimate approximate distance (based on the centre point of the postal area; an estimate only).
  • To send notifications such as new messages, ride cancellations, or removal from a ride.
  • To verify in-app purchases and activate the purchased service.
  • To keep the platform safe: automatically filter inappropriate language, handle reports, and act on accounts that violate our Terms.
  • To respond to feedback and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. What other users can see

  • Your nickname and profile photo are visible to users you interact with.
  • Published coach profiles (including photos and social links) are visible to all users, along with an approximate distance.
  • Rides you post are visible to all users; once you join a ride, your nickname and photo are visible to its members.
  • Messages are visible only to members of that conversation.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the following service providers, and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage. Data is stored on servers in the United States (Oregon).
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, push notifications (which may show up to the first 120 characters of a message, or the host's nickname, departure time and resort for ride alerts, on your lock screen), and in-app purchases.
  • Resend — email delivery used to send the contents of reports and feedback to our inbox. Its servers are in the United States.

Otherwise, we disclose information only when required by law, in response to a lawful request from authorities, or when necessary to protect someone's safety.

Cross-border transfer: because these providers are located in the United States, your information is stored and processed outside Canada and may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction, including lawful access by its authorities.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS). Database access is restricted per user so each user can only read and write data they are permitted to access. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; we may review relevant content when handling a report or when required by law. No system is completely secure.

6. Retention and account deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time in the app under Me → Delete Account. When you do:

  • Your account, profile, profile photo, coach profile and photos, rides you posted, saved coaches, block list, push tokens and feedback are deleted.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app's Sign in with Apple authorization.
  • Messages you sent are disassociated from your account and appear in the other party's conversation as sent by a deleted user.
  • Reports you submitted are retained, no longer linked to your account, for safety purposes.
  • Purchase transaction records are retained, no longer linked to your account, to prevent duplicate fulfilment and for financial records.

While your account exists, we keep the information above in order to provide the service.

7. Your rights

Under PIPEDA you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, and withdraw consent (which may mean you can no longer use some or all features). Most information can be edited directly in the app. For access or other requests, contact us at the address below; we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

8. Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you in the app and update the effective date at the top of this page.
